About that auto email sender

It's genuinely useful here, but for a narrower job than you'd think. The measured funnel is roughly one deal per 464 cold emails, and small-business owners answer the phone at 18–25% against a 3.4% email reply rate. Volume is the losing move.

  • Use it for: pitching editing and case-study clients — agencies, SMB marketing leads, indie authors, academics. Forty researched pitches beat four thousand blasts, and it's the one place the tool compounds.
  • Don't use it for: the newsletter. beehiiv and Substack handle sending, list management, and unsubscribes natively, and their deliverability reputation is worth more than yours will be.
  • Keep it legal: CAN-SPAM applies to every commercial send — accurate headers, honest subject lines, a real physical postal address, a working opt-out honored within 10 days. Penalties run per email, not per campaign.
  • Warm the domain: 10 sends/day the first week, 20 the second, never past ~50/day on one domain. Ramping straight to volume gets you filtered permanently, and you don't get that reputation back.
